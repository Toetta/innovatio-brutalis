Innovatio Brutalis helps businesses in Malmö, Skåne and southern Sweden move from needs, concepts and system architecture to CAD, mechatronics, functional physical and connected prototypes, verification and low-volume production.

From idea to physical and connected solution

Innovation needs to work in practice. Industrial design and engineering guide the choice of process. Rapid prototyping supports tests and iteration before a small production run.

  • Needs analysis, requirements, and risks
  • Problem definition & functional breakdown
  • Concepts and system architecture
  • CAD and mechanical design with DFM in mind
  • Electronics and embedded software
  • Arduino / ESP32 prototypes
  • Sensors, actuators and communication
  • Simulation & technical analysis
  • 3D printing / CO₂ laser cutting and engraving / CNC machining
  • Functional prototypes
  • Testing and verification
  • Iteration based on test results
  • One-offs & small series (metal and engineering polymers)

A prototype does not have to stop at mechanics. Innovatio Brutalis also develops mechatronics prototypes combining mechanics, electronics, firmware and communication – for example ESP32/Arduino, sensors, actuators, e-ink displays, RFID/NFC, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi.

Mechatronics & embedded prototyping connect the physical product with control and app communication.

The right manufacturing method for each part of the prototype. In-house 3D printing, CNC machining, CO₂ laser cutting, 3D scanning and mechatronics connect digital design with physical verification. A product does not have to be designed around one manufacturing method.
