CNC Machining

4-axis mechanical machining and probing — from prototype to low-volume production.

Based in Malmö, working with businesses in Skåne and southern Sweden. CNC machining connects design to functional prototypes and product development.

CNC is mechanical subtractive machining for metals, engineering plastics, 3D geometry and precision parts. CO₂ laser cutting mainly serves flat laser-suitable materials such as acrylic and wood, panels and engraving, including longer material with conveyor feed. Need a laser-cut part?
